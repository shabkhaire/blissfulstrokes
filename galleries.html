For Galleries & Art Professionals

I am an artist based in Massachusetts, working in oil, acrylic and watercolour. My work explores nature, light, color, memory, culture, and the quiet moments of everyday life.

I am currently participating in art shows and exhibitions and interested in connecting with galleries, curators, artists, and art communities for exhibitions and creative collaborations.

Practice

Painting in oil, acrylic and watercolour. Work is often developed from the artist’s own photographs and close observation.

Open to

Solo and group exhibitions, juried shows, curated online features, artist talks, workshops, and collaborations with other artists.
